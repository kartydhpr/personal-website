Privacy

This site is a personal portfolio run by Karteikay Dhuper. It does not ask you to log in and does not intentionally collect names or email addresses from casual visitors.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic (for example which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are located, and what devices browsers report). That processing is described in Google’s privacy policy. You can use browser settings or common ad/analytics blocking tools if you prefer not to be included in those statistics.

Hosting
