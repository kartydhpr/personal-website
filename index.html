Art and Design

I believe that good design connects people to the product, whether it be a video game, a mobile app, or a website — the design and aesthetics are what make the connection between the user and the product more meaningful and enjoyable.

This is why I find it helpful to utilize my background in art and design to help visualize my ideas before getting them off the page into something more tangible — like this website. In my free time I like to illustrate anything from mundane household objects to space and interstellar phenomena.
